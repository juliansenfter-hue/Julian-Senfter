Features

Everything you need
to build great habits

Simple, beautiful tools that make showing up every day feel rewarding — not like a chore.

One-tap check-ins

Mark habits done in a single tap. Satisfying animations celebrate every completion and keep you motivated to keep going.

Streak tracking

Watch your streaks grow day by day. Lose your streak and it resets — the perfect motivator to never miss a day again.

Progress insights

Beautiful charts show your completion rates, best streaks, and patterns — helping you understand yourself better over time.

Smart reminders

Gentle nudges at exactly the right time. HabitFlow learns when you're most likely to complete habits and reminds you then.

Friend challenges

Invite friends and compete on shared habits. A little friendly competition is the best accountability partner you'll ever have.

AI coaching

Get personalized advice based on your habit data. Your AI coach identifies patterns and suggests improvements unique to you.
